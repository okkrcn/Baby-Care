## Description (4000 char limit)

```
Baby Care is a free, privacy-first app for tracking your baby's care from birth to age 2. All your data stays on your device — no cloud, no account, no ads.

🍼 FEEDING
• Breastfeeding (left/right + duration) and bottle feeding (ml) entries
• Live breastfeeding timer — start and stop whenever
• Daily totals for time and amount

😴 SLEEP
• Completed or in-progress sleep records
• Day nap vs. night sleep tracking
• Live sleep timer

🍼 DIAPER
• Pee, poo, mixed entries
• Stool consistency (loose, normal, hard) options

💉 VACCINATION
• Turkish Ministry of Health 2026 Expanded Immunization Program (hexavalent DTaP-IPV-Hib-HepB included)
• Auto-generated schedule from birth date
• Reminder notifications: 3 days before, 1 day before, day of vaccine
• Mark complete + add notes

📏 GROWTH CHARTS
• Weight, height, head circumference measurements
• World Health Organization (WHO) percentile curves (female/male)
• See exactly which band your baby falls in
• Perfect for pediatric checkups

📖 DEVELOPMENT GUIDE
• Age-appropriate gross motor, fine motor, language, social-emotional expectations
• Feeding and sleep tips
• Warning signs (when to see a doctor)

💧 VITAMIN & MEDICATION
• Vitamin D added automatically per Ministry of Health recommendation (400 IU/day)
• Daily reminder notifications
• Add any vitamin or medication you need

📄 PDF REPORT
• Generate a pediatric checkup report with one tap
• Includes baby info + growth table + vaccination status + medications
• Share via email, message, or AirDrop

👶 MULTIPLE BABIES
• Separate profiles for twins or siblings
• Quick switch from the top toolbar

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• No data collected, nothing sent to any server
• No account, email, or registration required
• No ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs
• Delete all your data anytime

📱 OPTIMIZED FOR iOS 26
• Liquid Glass design system
• Native SwiftUI performance
• iPhone and iPad compatible
• Turkish language support

⚠️ IMPORTANT
The content in this app is for informational purposes only and does not replace advice from healthcare professionals. For decisions about your baby's health, please consult your pediatrician.

SOURCES
• Turkish Ministry of Health Expanded Immunization Program
• World Health Organization Child Growth Standards
• American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) developmental guides
```

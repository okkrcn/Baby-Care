# Privacy Policy — Baby Care

**Last updated:** June 18, 2026

## Summary

Baby Care **does not collect, share, or transmit any data** about you or your baby. All information is stored only on your device.

## Data Collected

No personal data is collected. The app does not send data to any server, does not use analytics services, does not connect to advertising networks, and does not share information with third parties.

## Data Stored on Device

While using the app, you save the following information locally on your device:

- Baby's name, birth date, sex, birth weight/height
- Feeding, sleep, and diaper tracking records
- Vaccination schedule and completion status
- Growth measurements (weight, height, head circumference)
- Vitamin and medication tracking
- User notes

This data is stored only in your device's local database (Apple SwiftData). If iCloud Backup is enabled, the system may include this data in your encrypted iCloud Backup, as managed by Apple — this is outside Baby Care's control.

## Notifications

With your permission, the app only sends local (on-device) notifications:

- Vaccination reminders (3 days before, 1 day before, day of)
- Daily vitamin/medication reminders

Notifications are generated entirely on your device and never originate from a server. You can disable them at any time via Settings → Notifications.

## Children's Privacy

This app is intended for parents of newborns (0–6 months). No information about the baby is sent over the internet. No adult user account, email address, or registration is required.

## Data Deletion

You can permanently delete all your data at any time via Settings → "Reset All Data". Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.

## Medical Disclaimer

All content in the app (vaccination schedules, developmental guides, percentile charts, feeding tips) is for informational purposes only. It does not replace professional medical advice. For any decisions regarding your baby's health, please consult your pediatrician or local family health center.

Sources: WHO Child Growth Standards, AAP developmental guidelines, and Turkish Ministry of Health Expanded Immunization Program.

## Third-Party SDKs and Services

None. The app uses only Apple's system frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, UserNotifications, Charts).

## Changes

When this policy is updated, the new version will be published on the App Store listing alongside the updated app release.
